El Curioso Uploader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Who we are

El Curioso Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "El Curioso" (https://www.youtube.com/@ElCurioso18) to upload the owner's own original videos and thumbnails to that same channel.

2. YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

3. Google Privacy Policy

Google's Privacy Policy applies to the data handled through YouTube API Services: http://www.google.com/policies/privacy

4. What data is accessed and how it is used

  • The channel owner's own Google account (OAuth), to upload videos and set thumbnails on their own channel.
  • Public video statistics (views, durations, titles), to see which topics are popular. Only aggregate numbers are kept.

No personal data of any other user is collected.

5. Storage and sharing

The sign-in token and the statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer and in the owner's private work environment. No data is sold, shared with third parties or shown to anyone else.

6. Data deletion

  • Public statistics obtained from the API are refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revoking, the stored token is deleted.
  • To request deletion of any data, contact the channel owner; it will be deleted within 7 days.

7. Contact

Through the channel's About page: https://www.youtube.com/@ElCurioso18/about
